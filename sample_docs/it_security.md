# Acme Corp IT Security Policy (sample, fictional)
Always connect through the corporate VPN when using public wifi.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for email and the HR portal.
Report phishing emails using the "Report Phish" button or forward them to security@acme.example.
Laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption; lost devices must be reported within 24 hours.
