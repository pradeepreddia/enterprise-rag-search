# Acme Corp HR Handbook (sample, fictional)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls over to the next year.
Timesheets must be submitted every Friday by 5 PM Pacific. Managers approve timesheets by Monday noon.
Parental leave is 12 weeks fully paid for all new parents.
Expense reports over $500 need director approval and receipts attached.
